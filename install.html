TCG Undercut

Version 0.4.0

A bookmark button for your TCGplayer seller portal. It finds your in-stock listings that cost buyers more than the cheapest listing, counting shipping, and once you confirm, lowers them so your total is $0.01 under it.

Install it once

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl + Shift + B in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar:
    TCG Undercut

Replacing an older TCG Undercut bookmark? Right-click the old one and choose Delete first. The panel shows its version next to its name, so you can check which one you're running.

Use it

  1. In the seller portal, open Inventory (store.tcgplayer.com/admin/product/catalog).
  2. Click TCG Undercut on the bookmarks bar. It reads every product in your inventory, which takes a few seconds. Clicked on another TCGplayer page, it offers to open Inventory for you; on a blank new tab the browser doesn't run it at all.
  3. Review the table and untick anything you want to leave alone.
  4. Click Apply, then Yes, change prices. Keep the tab open while it saves; the footer counts progress, and your browser asks before letting you leave. Each new price is read back from TCGplayer to confirm it took.

How it decides

  • It compares what a buyer pays, price + shipping, because that's how TCGplayer sorts listings. Shipping is counted the way TCGplayer's Match button counts it: if the cheapest listing's price is under $5, your shipping counts as at least the $1.49 minimum; at $5 or more, it's what you charge.
  • When your total is higher, the new price puts it $0.01 under the cheapest total. If the cheapest is $13.00 + $1.49 shipping and you ship that card free, your new price is $14.48. If the cheapest is $5.00 with free shipping and you're at $5.20, it offers $4.99.
  • A cut is marked Big drop and starts unticked when it is 10% or more and your current price is $10.00 or more, in case the lowest listing is a mistake. For example, $50.39 → $40.29 (20%) is flagged, while $57.27 → $54.96 (4%) and $0.81 → $0.65 (20%, but under $10) stay ticked.
  • Right before saving, each product is read again. If its quantity, your price or the lowest listing changed since the scan, that row isn't saved and you're asked to rescan.

What it won't do

  • It never raises a price or changes a quantity. Each save sends back the quantity it has just re-read.
  • It never undercuts your own listing. Listings already at or below the lowest are left alone, and a lowest listing at exactly your price is treated as yours.
  • It never touches photo listings, out-of-stock items, or anything except the TCGplayer Marketplace price.

What the results mean

  • ✓ Saved at $…: TCGplayer now shows the new price.
  • ⚠ Changed since the scan, so not saved: the quantity, your price or the lowest listing moved after the table was built. Rescan to see that listing again.
  • ⚠ Sent, but TCGplayer still shows the old price: the save went out, but the new price hadn't appeared a few seconds later. Rescan in a minute; if it still shows the old price, change it on that product's Manage page.
  • ✗ and a reason: that save failed. Check the product's Manage page before trying again.
  • Couldn't be read, below the table: those products weren't checked, so none of their prices were changed.

Limits

  • It relies on how TCGplayer's seller pages work today. If TCGplayer changes them, the affected products show up as couldn't be read instead of being repriced.
  • It reads one page per product, so a large inventory takes a while: a few seconds per 10 products.
  • It doesn't work with staged inventory or with accounts that also sell through a My Store channel, and says so if you try.

It runs in your browser with your existing TCGplayer sign-in and talks only to TCGplayer. It is unofficial and not affiliated with TCGplayer.
